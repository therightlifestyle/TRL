    What this demonstrates

    A first pass, made visible

    The logic below is deliberately simple and visible — the kind of first pass a real system would apply before anything is handed to a person. In a live engagement this becomes a connected workflow: capture into a register, notify the right owner, track the follow-up date, and log the outcome.

    • ProblemEnquiries arrive as free text. Details are lost, follow-ups depend on memory, and nothing is recorded in a form you can act on later.
    • ApproachStructure the message into consistent fields, apply simple routing rules, produce a draft reply for human approval.
    • DeliverableA working prototype plus the logic behind it.
    • StatusDemonstration. Not client work, not a measured result, and not a finished product.
