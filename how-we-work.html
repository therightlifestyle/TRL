Process

A process designed so that nothing is assumed.

Most project problems are definition problems that were never caught. The process below exists to catch them early — before money, before building, and before anyone is disappointed.

The engagement

Six stages

01

Conversation

A short discussion about what is happening in the business and where the friction actually is. No pitch, and no obligation.

02

Definition

The problem, the scope and the outcome are written down. Work starts only when both sides agree on what success looks like.

03

Confirmation

Scope, price, timeline and payment terms are confirmed in writing before any money changes hands.

04

Build

The work is delivered in visible stages, with the reasoning explained rather than hidden behind progress updates.

05

Review

You review the work against the agreed scope. Anything that misses the mark is corrected before handover.

06

Handover

You receive the deliverable, the documentation and a walkthrough, so the system can be run without us.

Commercial terms

Stated plainly, before you ask

  • Audit pricePKR 3,000, fixed. Published openly.
  • Payment — audits100% in advance. Work starts once payment is confirmed as received.
  • Payment — projects50% before work begins, with the balance agreed in writing before delivery.
  • Delivery — auditWithin 72 hours of confirmed payment.
  • Delivery — projectsAgreed in writing per project. We do not commit to a date before the scope is understood.
  • AdjustmentsA seven-day window after an audit is delivered, to correct anything factually wrong about your business.
  • Changes to scopeQuoted separately in writing. We do not absorb silent scope creep, and we do not spring charges on you.
  • ImplementationAlways quoted separately. The audit fee is not credited against a build.

Payment is confirmed by checking that funds have actually been credited, not from a screenshot alone. This protects both sides.

Boundaries

What we will not do

  • Access private data unnecessarilyAudits use public information and whatever you choose to provide. We do not ask for passwords.
  • Promise specific outcomesResults depend on your process and your market. We will not quote a percentage improvement we cannot stand behind.
  • Present a prototype as finishedA demonstration is labelled a demonstration. A prototype is not sold as a production system.
  • Claim work we have not doneNo invented clients, testimonials, case studies, awards or metrics — on our site or on yours.
  • Sell work that is not neededSometimes the right answer is to do nothing yet. We will say so.
