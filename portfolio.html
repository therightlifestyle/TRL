Why show this

An early-stage company has to earn trust differently.

We could fill this page with stock imagery and vague claims about transformation. It would be easier, and it would not tell you anything useful.

What tells you something useful is a working thing you can open, press, and judge for yourself. If the standard of thinking in the demonstration is not what you are looking for, you will know within a minute — and neither of us will have wasted a meeting.
