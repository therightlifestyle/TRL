Privacy notice

How TRL — The Right Lifestyle handles personal information. Last updated 19 October 2026.

This notice explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is written to be readable rather than to look thorough.

Who this applies to

TRL — The Right Lifestyle is a founder-led service company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. This notice covers our website, our correspondence, and the work we deliver.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we hold that message and your contact details so we can reply and, if you become a client, deliver the work.
  • Project information. For active projects we hold the minimum needed: contact details, requirements, agreed scope and payment records.
  • Nothing else is collected on this website. There are no analytics, no advertising trackers, no cookies and no third-party scripts anywhere on this site.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, to deliver agreed work, to meet record-keeping obligations, and to keep a record of what was agreed. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted once it is clear there is no work to do. Project records are kept for as long as needed to deliver the work and meet legal or tax obligations, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 319 0091457 or email officialtrlservice@gmail.com and we will action it.

Security

We keep access limited and use reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, so we hold the minimum information needed rather than everything we could.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.

This is a starter notice prepared in good faith. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Local legal review is recommended before relying on it.
