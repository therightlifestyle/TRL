Terms of engagement

The standard basis on which TRL — The Right Lifestyle accepts work. Last updated 19 October 2026.

These terms set out how work is agreed, paid for and delivered. Where a signed proposal or agreement says something different, that document takes precedence.

1 · Scope comes first

No work begins before the scope, deliverables, price and timeline are agreed in writing. If the scope changes, the change is quoted separately and agreed in writing before it is built.

2 · Pricing and payment

  • The Rapid AI Opportunity Audit is PKR 3,000, payable 100% in advance.
  • Implementation and project work is quoted in writing after the scope is understood.
  • Projects require 50% before work begins, with the balance agreed in writing before delivery.
  • Payment is treated as received only once funds are confirmed as credited.

3 · Delivery

Audits are delivered within 72 hours of confirmed payment. Project timelines are agreed per project in writing; we do not commit to a date before the scope is understood.

4 · Revisions

Audits include a seven-day adjustment window to correct anything factually wrong about your business. Project revisions are handled as defined in the written scope for that project.

5 · Implementation is separate

An audit is an assessment, not a build. Implementation is quoted separately against a defined scope, and the audit fee is not credited against it. There is no obligation to proceed.

6 · Confidentiality

Information you share with us is treated as confidential. We do not publish client work, results or identifying details without your written permission.

7 · Intellectual property

On full payment, you own the deliverables produced for your project — including source code, documentation and any accounts created for the work. We retain the right to describe the type of work performed, without disclosing confidential information, unless agreed otherwise in writing.

8 · Honest claims

We do not publish invented results, testimonials or metrics. We ask the same standard of material produced for clients. Where a figure on this site is modelled from information a visitor enters — for example in the opportunity model — it is labelled as a model, not as a result.

9 · Availability and support

Retainer support is month to month with 30 days' notice on either side, and a full handover of anything live at the point of exit. Scope not covered by the retainer is quoted separately before any work begins.
