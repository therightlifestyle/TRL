What we do

Seven capabilities, one operating principle: make the work hold up without you.

TRL is not a single-product agency. The capability is broad by design, because the businesses we work with rarely have one narrow problem. AI and automation often lead the conversation — they are rarely the whole answer.

01

AI and Automation

Repetitive work that depends on one person remembering every step: enquiries, order capture, confirmations, follow-ups and routine reporting.

  • Workflow automation
  • AI-assisted processes
  • practical AI implementation and handover documentation.
02

Websites and Digital Products

A business that needs a credible public presence, a focused landing page, or a small scoped digital product — without a long, open-ended build.

  • Company websites
  • landing pages
  • portfolio sites and scoped digital product work.
03

Research and Business Information

Decisions being made on assumptions rather than evidence: market structure, competitor behaviour, pricing signals, or a shortlist that needs verifying.

  • Market research
  • competitor research
  • structured information gathering and decision-support reports.
04

Business Strategy and Documentation

Processes that live in someone's head, or a business that needs its operations written down clearly enough to delegate or improve.

  • Planning documents
  • operating procedures
  • process design and business documentation.
05

Marketing, Branding and Content

Capability that is hard to explain to a customer, or marketing activity happening without a consistent message or repeatable system.

  • Brand materials
  • content systems
  • marketing collateral and campaign support.
06

Software, Integrations and Custom Solutions

Tools that do not talk to each other, manual double-entry between systems, or a specific job that no off-the-shelf tool handles.

  • Scoped software work
  • system integrations and tailored digital solutions.
07

Consultation and Strategic Guidance

A problem that needs a clear-thinking outside view before any money is spent on building anything.

  • Structured business discussions
  • problem diagnosis
  • planning and strategic guidance.

How the work runs

Four stages. No surprises, no hidden scope.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    A short, honest discussion about what is happening in the business and what is actually causing the friction.

  2. 02

    Definition

    The problem, the scope and the outcome are written down. Both sides agree on what success looks like before work begins.

  3. 03

    Build

    The work is done in visible stages, with the reasoning explained rather than hidden behind progress updates.

  4. 04

    Handover

    You receive the deliverable, the documentation, and a walkthrough so the system can be run without us.
