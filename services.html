What each capability covers, and when it applies.

Seven disciplines, described honestly — including what each one is not for. Most engagements use two or three of them together, because real operational problems do not respect department boundaries.

01

AI and Automation

Repetitive work that depends on one person remembering every step: enquiries, order capture, confirmations, follow-ups and routine reporting. We automate the repeatable — never the responsible.

What you get

  • Enquiry intake with automatic classification, routing and SLA clocks
  • AI-assisted drafting, with a human approval step before anything is sent
  • Integrations between the tools the business already pays for
  • Scheduled jobs: digests, reminders, reconciliations, exception reports
  • A runbook and monitoring for every workflow shipped

Tools we usually reach for

  • WhatsApp Business, Google Workspace, n8n, Make
  • OpenAI API, Google Apps Script, Airtable, Supabase

What it changes

  • Fewer enquiries waiting for a first reply
  • No double entry between systems
  • Every repeat task traceable and auditable

Not for

  • Work that needs human judgement, taste or accountability
  • Replacing a platform you have just paid to implement
See the triage engine
02

Websites and Digital Products

A business that needs a credible public presence, a focused landing page, a client portal, or a small scoped digital product — without a long, open-ended build.

What you get

  • Brand, structure and content architecture before any pixel work
  • A responsive build with Core Web Vitals in budget
  • Enquiry capture wired into the inbox your team already uses
  • A self-hosted stack with no tracking and no lock-in
  • Documentation, deployment pipeline and handover training

How we build

  • HTML, modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no page-builder debt
  • Netlify or Cloudflare for hosting, GitHub Actions for deployment
  • Design tokens as a single source of truth

What it changes

  • Fast on a mid-range phone and on a poor connection
  • Enquiries land where the team already works

Not for

  • Full identity programmes with no in-house brand owner
  • Sites that need a content team to stay alive
See website work
03

Research and Business Information

Decisions being made on assumptions rather than evidence: market structure, competitor behaviour, pricing signals, or a shortlist that needs verifying before money moves.

What you get

  • A competitor and pricing landscape with source links
  • Market sizing with every assumption stated
  • Tool evaluations against your actual constraints
  • A feasibility brief with one clear recommendation
  • The raw research files, not just the summary

How it runs

  • Fixed fee, 5–10 working days
  • Questions answered before the research starts
  • Findings written for an owner, not for an analyst

Not for

  • Paid databases and large-sample primary research
  • Market sizing with no intention of acting on it
04

Business Strategy and Documentation

Processes that live in someone's head, or a business that needs its operations written down clearly enough to delegate, improve, or sell.

What you get

  • An as-is process map with the friction points marked
  • Standard operating procedures for the work that matters
  • A decision log: what was decided, when, why and by whom
  • An ownership and escalation matrix
  • A handover pack a new hire can follow on day one

How it runs

  • Usually one to three weeks, standalone or as part of a build
  • Written with your team, not for them
  • Reviewed against the real work, not the intention

What it changes

  • The founder can take leave without the business stalling
  • New staff onboard faster and with fewer errors

Not for

  • Strategy decks that will never be read or acted on
  • Documenting a process the business has decided to end
05

Marketing, Branding and Content

Capability that is hard to explain to a customer, or marketing happening without a consistent message, a repeatable production line, or a way to capture what comes back.

What you get

  • A message framework: who it is for, and why they should care
  • Core brand assets with a one-page usage guide
  • A publishing calendar and production workflow
  • Landing pages wired into the enquiry pipeline
  • Follow-up sequences that sound like a person

Engagement

  • Monthly retainer or a defined campaign build
  • Content system first, volume second

What it changes

  • Publishing that happens without heroics
  • Leads captured with context attached

Not for

  • Volume content production for brands needing an in-house editorial team
06

Software, Integrations and Custom Solutions

Tools that do not talk to each other, manual double entry between systems, or a specific job that no off-the-shelf tool handles and never will.

What you get

  • Integration design with error handling and retries
  • Custom scripts and webhooks with structured logging
  • Spreadsheet automations for Google Sheets and Excel
  • Documentation, credential handover and a rollback plan
  • Monitoring, so silent failures are caught

Stack

  • Node.js, Python, Google Apps Script
  • REST APIs, webhooks, scheduled jobs

What it changes

  • One entry, one source of truth
  • No re-keying between systems

Not for

  • Replacing a system the business just paid to implement
See the architecture
07

Consultation and Strategic Guidance

A problem that needs a clear-thinking outside view before any money is spent building anything — or a business that needs a fractional systems leader rather than a full-time one.

What you get

  • A structured diagnostic of the real operating problem
  • A written recommendation with options and trade-offs
  • Vendor evaluation criteria and a shortlist
  • Architecture advice for the tools you already own
  • Fractional leadership, if that is genuinely what you need

Engagement

  • Single sessions, the fixed-fee audit, or ongoing advisory
  • Written in your language, not ours

Not for

  • Advice we cannot stand behind

If the honest answer is “you do not need this”, that is the answer you get — and it comes with a written explanation.
