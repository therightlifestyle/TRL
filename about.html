How we define scope

Start from the problem, not the technology.

It is tempting to start with a solution — a chatbot, a dashboard, an automation. We start by understanding what is actually slowing the business down, because the expensive mistakes are made when a tool is bought before the problem is defined.

That is also why the first engagement is a fixed-scope audit rather than an open-ended project. It is cheaper for you, and it forces both sides to be specific.

The five-layer architecture we design toward

  • Capture — every enquiry enters through one door, with the original message intact.
  • Route — every enquiry is classified, scored and assigned, with an SLA clock running.
  • Automate — repetitive steps run on rules, with a human approving anything outbound.
  • Document — every process and decision written down in a form a new hire can follow.
  • Review — service levels, exception reporting and honest improvement cycles.

Commitments

What you can hold us to.

Written scope

Nothing starts before the scope, price and timeline are written down and agreed. What is out of scope is written down too.

Honest labelling

Demonstrations are labelled as demonstrations. We do not present a prototype as a finished product, and we do not publish metrics we cannot stand behind.

No invented credentials

No fabricated clients, results, testimonials, awards or team members — on our site or yours. Where a client is named, it is with written permission.

Explained reasoning

You get the reasoning behind a recommendation, not just the recommendation. If we chose not to do something, we explain why.

Confidentiality

Client information, prospect details and internal material are never published or reused without written permission.

A real no

If the work is not worth doing, we will tell you before you spend anything. That answer comes with a written explanation.

How the company works

Small on purpose, and honest about it.

We are a founder-led company, not an agency with a bench. That has a clear upside — you get the person who will do the work, on the call, from the first message.

It also has a limit. We do not have a designer, a copywriter, a data scientist and a security engineer on staff. When a project needs one of those, we say so and either bring in a trusted specialist we have worked with, or tell you that the work should stay with you.
