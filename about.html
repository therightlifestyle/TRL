About

A company built around a simple observation: most businesses run on memory.

TRL — The Right Lifestyle is a founder-led service company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. We help owner-operated businesses turn the work that only one person knows how to do into systems that hold up when that person is not in the room.

Rashid Muhammad founded the company to work on exactly that problem. The company is founder-led and deliberately so: the person you speak to at the start is the person doing the work at the end.

How we define scope

Start from the problem, not the technology.

It is tempting to start with a solution — a chatbot, a dashboard, an automation. We start by understanding what is actually slowing the business down, because the expensive mistakes are made when a tool is bought before the problem is defined.

That is also why the first engagement is a fixed-scope audit rather than an open-ended project. It is cheaper for you, and it forces both sides to be specific.
